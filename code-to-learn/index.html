Code to Learn

Mon, Oct 6, 2025 · Rob

Listening to the Ed-Technical podcast with an interview with Dr. Jason Yeatman of Stanford University.

The thing that really popped out at me, which probably should be surprising to any early childhood educators was “you stop learning to read and you start to read to learn”.

Made me think of my field of Digital Technologies education and how it feels like the majority of our work, apart from some rare cases with motivated students, feels like from K-12 we’re just “learning to code” and never reach the stage of “coding to learn”.

Probably quite applicable to other fields as well like art and drawing. Teaching mathematics and very occasionally concepts like 3D design and 2D drafting to students for printing and cutting, the idea of “learn to draw” vs “draw to learn” is also pretty apparent.

Let’s first teach kids to code, and to think algorithmically, and once we’ve got a good start with that we’ll try to figure out how to make this stuff relevant to how they spend the other hours of their school day.
